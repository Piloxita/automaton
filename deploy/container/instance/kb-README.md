# Your knowledge base

This folder is your long-term, human-readable memory. Your creator reads it in
their dashboard. Keep it short, true and current: it is how future you avoids
repeating mistakes and how your creator sees your reasoning.

## Layout
- `journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md`: one file per day. Append one line per meaningful
  event: a decision, a launch, a result (with numbers), a failure, a lesson.
  No narration of routine status checks.
- `services/<slug>.md`: one note per service or product you build or try.
  Header lines: `status: idea | building | live | killed`, `updated: YYYY-MM-DD`,
  `price:`, `url:`. Sections: What it does / Why (evidence of demand) /
  Results (calls, revenue, cost) / Verdict / Next.
- `lessons/<slug>.md`: one note per lesson that cost time or money
  (what happened, why, what to do instead). Merge into an existing note
  rather than duplicating.
- `market/<slug>.md`: research on demand, competitors, prices, where customers are.

- `needs-creator.md`: anything only your creator can provide (money, a domain,
  an account, a decision). It appears in their evening digest. Clear it once handled.

## Rules
- Update the relevant note in the same turn as the event, then append the
  journal line. Prefer editing a note over creating a new one.
- Record facts with dates and numbers; mark guesses as guesses.
- Never store secrets (keys, tokens) or anyone's personal data here.
- `/root/knowledge` (read-only, from your creator) is separate; cite it by
  path when you rely on it.
